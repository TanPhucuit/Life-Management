# NEUTRON STAR (PULSAR) VISUALIZATION SPECIFICATION

This visualization theme represents a rapidly rotating Pulsar (a highly magnetized neutron star).

This is NOT a normal star.

Do NOT render it like the Sun.

Avoid fireball aesthetics.

Avoid orange flames.

Avoid cartoon plasma.

The entire scene should feel like an extremely dense object that continuously emits electromagnetic energy.

Visual references:

- Interstellar
- NASA neutron star visualization
- ESA magnetar visualization
- Foundation
- Love Death & Robots

------------------------------------------------------------

# OBJECT SCALE

The neutron star should appear physically small.

Approximately:

Star Radius

≈ 1 unit

Planet Orbit

≈ 15~40 units

This immediately creates the feeling that the object is incredibly dense.

------------------------------------------------------------

# STAR APPEARANCE

The star should consist of multiple rendering layers.

Layer 1
Core Sphere

Pure white.

Extremely bright.

Almost saturated.

Soft blue edge.

No visible terrain.

Very smooth sphere.

------------------------------------------------------------

Layer 2
Surface Plasma

Animated procedural shader.

High frequency noise.

Fast flowing energy.

No flames.

The plasma should appear trapped beneath the surface.

Movement direction continuously changes.

Speed should be much higher than solar plasma.

------------------------------------------------------------

Layer 3
Outer Glow

Large HDR bloom.

Blue-white.

Intensity changes slightly over time.

Should pulse with rotation.

------------------------------------------------------------

Layer 4
Electric Corona

Thin electrical arcs randomly appear.

Lifetime

100~300 ms

Random positions.

Travel across the surface.

Disappear.

Never synchronize.

------------------------------------------------------------

Layer 5
Magnetic Ribbon System

This is the most important visual element.

Generate dozens of magnetic field ribbons.

They originate from one magnetic pole.

Curve naturally.

Return to the opposite pole.

Use spline geometry.

Tube geometry.

Animated shader.

Very subtle transparency.

Blue / cyan.

Continuously oscillate.

The ribbons should never become static.

Think Aurora.

Not lasers.

------------------------------------------------------------

Layer 6
Polar Jets

Both poles continuously emit narrow plasma jets.

The jets should be:

Long

Thin

Highly emissive

Volumetric

Animated with flowing noise.

The jets extend far beyond the visible scene.

------------------------------------------------------------

Layer 7
Pulsar Beam

A second layer independent from the plasma jets.

Two wide cones of light.

Semi transparent.

Soft edges.

Rotate together with the neutron star.

The beam should behave like a lighthouse.

Not a laser.

------------------------------------------------------------

# IDLE ANIMATION

Rotation

Fast.

Continuous.

No stopping.

Surface plasma

Always moving.

Magnetic ribbons

Always oscillating.

Outer glow

Breathing.

Very subtle.

Electric arcs

Random.

Polar jets

Constant.

Nothing should ever become static.

------------------------------------------------------------

# TOPIC TRANSITION

The scene never changes.

Camera never teleports.

No fade.

No scene reload.

Everything happens inside one continuous simulation.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE 1

MAGNETIC INSTABILITY

Duration

800ms

Surface plasma speed

100%

↓

300%

Electric arcs

Increase.

Magnetic ribbons

Oscillation amplitude increases.

Glow intensity

+30%

Polar jets

Become brighter.

Planets remain stable.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE 2

ENERGY ACCUMULATION

Duration

1200ms

Surface begins developing extremely bright hotspots.

Hotspots expand.

Merge.

Separate.

Magnetic ribbons become chaotic.

Field lines twist.

Some reconnect.

Electric arcs become more frequent.

Star brightness gradually increases.

Camera performs a very slow dolly in.

Approximately 5%.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE 3

MAGNETIC RECONNECTION

This is the critical moment.

Multiple magnetic ribbons suddenly reconnect.

The field topology changes.

Large plasma loops collapse.

Huge amounts of energy are released.

Do NOT create an explosion.

Instead.

Create a feeling that magnetic tension has snapped.

Visual cues:

Light flashes.

Ribbon distortion.

Rapid electric discharge.

Temporary overexposure.

Camera micro shake.

------------------------------------------------------------

PHASE 4

MAGNETAR BURST

The neutron star emits one gigantic electromagnetic pulse.

This is NOT a fire explosion.

This is NOT a shockwave made of smoke.

Instead.

Generate an expanding spherical shell of light.

Characteristics:

Transparent.

Blue-white.

Refraction shader.

Heat distortion.

Volumetric edge.

No fire.

No debris.

Expansion speed

Extremely fast.

The pulse travels through the entire planetary system.

------------------------------------------------------------

PLANET REACTION

Each planet reacts only AFTER the pulse reaches it.

Never synchronize explosions.

For every planet:

State 1

Surface vibration.

State 2

Tiny rocks begin floating.

State 3

Internal cracks appear.

Blue-white light leaks through.

State 4

Pressure increases.

Planet slightly inflates.

State 5

Violent explosion.

Thousands of fragments.

------------------------------------------------------------

FRAGMENT SIMULATION

Large fragments

Remain solid.

Medium fragments

Spin.

Small fragments

Become glowing dust.

Very small fragments

Become ionized plasma.

Eventually disappear.

------------------------------------------------------------

PARTICLE SYSTEM

Use GPU particles.

Different particle classes:

Rock

Dust

Ionized Dust

Plasma

Spark

Glow

All particles inherit explosion velocity.

Then gradually slow down.

------------------------------------------------------------

LIGHTING

During the burst.

Exposure increases.

Bloom doubles.

Lens flare briefly appears.

Stars in the background become overexposed.

After the pulse passes.

Exposure gradually returns.

------------------------------------------------------------

CAMERA

Camera never cuts.

Never teleports.

Camera remains focused on the neutron star.

Camera choreography:

Energy accumulation

↓

Slow dolly in

↓

Magnetic reconnection

↓

Micro shake

↓

Burst

↓

Temporary exposure increase

↓

Shockwave crosses camera

↓

Camera settles

Everything should feel like a scientific observation rather than an action movie.

------------------------------------------------------------

NEW SYSTEM CREATION

After every old planet is destroyed.

Magnetic activity calms.

Surface brightness decreases.

Electric arcs become rare.

Magnetic ribbons stabilize.

Small glowing dust clouds appear.

Dust slowly condenses.

Molten spheres form.

Cooling crust develops.

Planets complete one by one.

The user should feel that a completely new planetary system has naturally emerged from the remains of the previous one.
